3. Why pure position→quat failed on our rig
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SMPL-X_Armature bone tails mostly point +Z (display), NOT along the limb.
  Rest directions must come from parent/child joint heads or from MoMask BVH IK,
  not bone head→tail vectors.

  Correct product path:
    MoMask joints → MoMask BVH (official IK) → constraint bake → SMPL-X Action

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. How to apply (when you resume body-from-text)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  # A) joints NPZ → BVH (momask venv)
  .\third_party\momask_venv\Scripts\python.exe tools\momask_joints_to_bvh.py `
    --npz "lmm train\momask_walk.npz" --out "lmm train\momask_walk.bvh" --ik

  # B) BVH → SMPL-X Action
  blender.exe whole_body_retargeted.blend --python tools\apply_lmm_npz_to_smplx.py -- `
    --bvh "lmm train\momask_walk.bvh" --action momask_walk `
    --out body_motion\_momask_applied.blend
